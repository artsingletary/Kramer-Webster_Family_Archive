Type: census

Date: 1910

Location: Deal Island District 14, Somerset County, Maryland, United States

1929 Commemorative Marriage Certificate – Adam Kramer and Sue C. Webster

Type: marriage_certificate

Date: 1929-07-27

Location: Maryland, United States

Decorative marriage certificate commemorating the marriage of Adam Kramer of Baltimore, Maryland, and Sue C. Webster of Baltimore, Maryland, on July 27, 1929. The certificate states that they were united in marriage according to the laws of Maryland. H. D. Stewart is identified as the officiant, and Mrs. H. D. Stewart is listed as a witness. The original document features floral illustrations and shows significant creasing, tears, and age-related deterioration. This certificate corroborates the marriage date and officiant recorded on the separate official marriage certificate.

People: Adam Johannes Krämer , Susie Catherine "Sue" Webster

1930 United States Federal Census – Adam Kramer and Snead Webster Families

Type: census

Date: 1930-04-02

Location: Ward 4, Baltimore City, Maryland, United States

The 1930 United States Federal Census records Adam Kramer (age 26) and his wife Sue Kramer (age 26) on lines 18–19. The following household, on lines 20–22, includes Snead Webster (age 58), Charles Webster (age 21), and Wilson Webster (age 10). Snead Webster was William Gabriel Snead Webster, father of Sue, Charles, and Wilson. The households were enumerated in Ward 4 of Baltimore City, Maryland, Enumeration District 4-232, Sheet 6A. The census records Sue as age 26, although her reported birth date of October 22, 1902, would make her 27 at the time of enumeration.

People: Susie Catherine "Sue" Webster , William Gabriel Snead Webster , Charles Daniel Webster , James Wilson Webster Sr.

1940 United States Federal Census – Adam Kramer Family

Type: census

Date: 1940-04-10

Location: Brentwood Avenue, Dundalk, Baltimore County, Maryland, United States

The 1940 United States Federal Census records the household of Adam Kramer (age 36), his wife Sue C. Kramer (age 36), and their children Robert A. Kramer and Betty A. Kramer. The family was enumerated on April 10, 1940, in Enumeration District 3-79, Sheet 7A, lines 34–37. Brentwood Avenue is handwritten vertically in column 1 of the census, spanning approximately lines 20–31. The Kramer family residence has also been identified as being on Brentwood Avenue in Dundalk, Baltimore County, Maryland, through firsthand family recollection. Betty A. Kramer is identified in family records as Bette Ann Kramer.

People: Adam Johannes Krämer , Susie Catherine "Sue" Webster , Robert Adam Kramer , Bette Ann Kramer

1993 Virginia Death Certificate – Sue C. Kramer

Type: death_certificate

Date: 1993-01-01

Location: Falls Church, Fairfax County, Virginia, United States

Commonwealth of Virginia Certificate of Death for Sue C. Kramer, who died on January 1, 1993, at Fairfax Hospital in Falls Church, Fairfax County, Virginia, at age 90. The certificate records her birth as October 22, 1902, in Maryland. Her father is identified as Gabriel Seneed and her mother as Addie Daniels, preserving the spellings recorded on the certificate. These names correspond to Gabriel Snead Webster and Addie Leola Daniel in the family archive. Her marital status is recorded as widowed, and Adam Kramer is named as her spouse. The certificate identifies Woodlawn Cemetery in Baltimore, Maryland, as her burial location. Bette Singletary is listed as the informant.

People: Susie Catherine "Sue" Webster , Adam Johannes Krämer , William Gabriel Snead Webster , Addie Leola Daniel
