1903 German Birth Certificate – Adam Johannes Krämer

Type: birth_certificate

Date: 1903-12-08

Location: Schweinfurt, Bavaria, Germany

German birth certificate (Geburtsurkunde) for Adam Johannes Krämer, documenting his birth on December 8, 1903, in Schweinfurt, Bavaria, Germany. The certificate identifies his father as Johannes Krämer and his mother as Anna Krämer, née Wolf. Both parents were recorded as Protestant and residing in Schweinfurt. The birth was recorded by Standesamt Schweinfurt under registration No. 554. This certified extract was issued on August 21, 1968. The certificate confirms Schweinfurt as Adam's birthplace, resolving the discrepancy with Würzburg recorded on his 1942 World War II draft registration.

1928 Declaration of Intention – Adam Kramer

Type: naturalization_record

Date: 1928-04-19

Location: Baltimore, Maryland, United States

United States Declaration of Intention filed by Adam Kramer on April 19, 1928, in the United States District Court for the District of Maryland. Adam identified himself as a 24-year-old seaman, born December 8, 1903, in Schweinfurt, Germany, and residing at 937 S. Kenwood Avenue, Baltimore, Maryland. He reported emigrating from Bremerhaven, Germany, aboard a vessel appearing to be named Derfflinger and arriving at New York on or about September 29, 1926. He identified his last foreign residence as Laufach, Germany, and stated that he was unmarried. The declaration records his intention to become a United States citizen.

1931 Petition for Citizenship – Adam Kramer

Type: naturalization_record

Date: 1931-11-27

Location: Baltimore, Maryland, United States

United States Petition for Citizenship No. 12778 filed by Adam Kramer on November 27, 1931, in the United States District Court at Baltimore, Maryland. Adam identified his residence as 104 South Curley Street, Baltimore, and his occupation as mechanical engineer. He reported being born on December 8, 1903, in Schweinfurt, Germany, and marrying Sue Kramer on July 27, 1929, in Ellicott City, Maryland. The petition records Sue's birthplace as Deal Island, Maryland, and states that she resided with him. Adam reported Laufach, Germany, as his last foreign residence and his arrival in New York on September 29, 1926, aboard the Derfflinger. He reported having no children at the time. The witnesses were Matilda Wiethger and Mrs. Sue Kramer. The petition was part of the naturalization proceedings that resulted in Adam becoming a United States citizen on May 19, 1932.

1942 World War II Draft Registration – Adam Kramer

Type: draft_registration

Date: 1942-02-14

Location: Baltimore County, Maryland, United States

World War II Selective Service registration card for Adam Kramer, registered on February 14, 1942, through Local Board No. 5, Baltimore County, Maryland. Adam reported his birth as December 8, 1903, in Würzburg, Germany, and his residence and mailing address as 6902 Brentwood Avenue, Baltimore, Maryland. He identified Sue Kramer of the same address as the person who would always know his whereabouts. His employer was Baltimore Pure-Rye Distilling Co., located on Sollers Point Road in Baltimore. The reverse of the card describes him as approximately 5 feet 8½ inches tall, with brown eyes, brown hair, and a light complexion. The birthplace differs from Schweinfurt, Germany, recorded in his 1928 Declaration of Intention; both locations are preserved as written in their respective records.

People: Adam Johannes Krämer

1969 Maryland Death Certificate – Adam Kramer

Type: death_certificate

Date: 1969-12-01

Location: Dundalk, Baltimore County, Maryland, United States

Maryland State Department of Health Certificate of Death for Adam Kramer, who died on December 1, 1969, at age 65 in Dundalk, Baltimore County, Maryland. The certificate records his birth as December 8, 1903, in Germany and identifies his residence as 6902 Brentwood Avenue, Dundalk. His father is named Johann Kramer and his mother Anna Wolff, preserving the spellings on this document. His German birth certificate identifies them as Johannes Krämer and Anna Wolf. The certificate records carcinoma of the lung with metastases to the brain as the cause of death, with diabetes mellitus listed as another significant condition. Sue Webster Kramer is identified as the informant. Burial took place on December 4, 1969, at Woodlawn Cemetery in Baltimore County, Maryland.
