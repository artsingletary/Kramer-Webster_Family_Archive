1910 United States Federal Census – William S. Webster Family

Type: census

Date: 1910

Location: Deal Island District 14, Somerset County, Maryland, United States

The 1910 United States Federal Census records the household of William S. Webster (age 38) and his wife Addie (age 28), along with their children Maynard S. (age 9), Susie C. (age 8), Florence N. (age 6), and Charles D. (age 9 months). William S. Webster was also known as William Snead Webster and later as Gabriel Snead Webster. The household was enumerated in Deal Island District 14, Somerset County, Maryland, Enumeration District 89, Sheet 3A.

People: William Gabriel Snead Webster , Addie Leola Daniel , Maynard Snead Webster , Susie Catherine "Sue" Webster , Florence Melson Webster , Charles Daniel Webster
