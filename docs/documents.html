1906 Land Deed – Addie L. Webster and Lazarus Benjamin Wilson

Type: deed

Date: 1906-08-20

Location: Deals Island, Somerset County, Maryland, United States

Deed dated August 20, 1906, in which Lazarus B. Wilson and his wife, Annie B. Wilson, of Baltimore City, Maryland, conveyed a parcel of land on Deals Island in Somerset County, Maryland, to Mrs. Addie L. Webster for $250. The property was described as part of a five-acre parcel formerly belonging to William Evans and land formerly belonging to Joseph Beauchamp. According to the Wilson-Daniel family tree, the grantor was Lazarus Benjamin Wilson (1857–1922), the maternal uncle of Addie Leola Daniel Webster (1881–1922). Lazarus and Addie's mother, Mary Catherine Wilson (1859–1910), were children of Samuel David Wilson (1823–1914). The deed documents a property transaction between Lazarus and Addie, while the family relationship is identified through genealogical research and awaits independent corroboration.

1930 United States Federal Census – Adam Kramer and Snead Webster Families

Type: census

Date: 1930-04-02

Location: Luzerne Avenue, Ward 4, Baltimore City, Maryland, United States

The Fifteenth Census of the United States, 1930, Maryland, Baltimore City, Ward 4, Enumeration District 4-532, Sheet 6A, enumerated April 2, 1930. Lines 18–19 record Adam Kramer (age 26) and his wife Sue Kramer (recorded age 26). Lines 20–22 record the immediately following household, headed by Snead Webster (age 58), with his sons Charles Webster (age 21) and Wilson Webster (age 10). Snead Webster was William Gabriel Snead Webster, father of Sue, Charles, and Wilson. The two households were located next to one another on Luzerne Avenue in Baltimore. The census records Sue as age 26, although her reported birth date of October 22, 1902, would make her 27 at the time of enumeration. This record establishes that Charles and Wilson were living in their father's household in April 1930, providing important context for research into the Webster children's living arrangements following the death of their mother, Addie Leola Daniel Webster, in 1922.

1931 Petition for Citizenship – Adam Kramer

Type: naturalization_record

Date: 1931-11-27

Location: Baltimore, Maryland, United States

United States Petition for Citizenship No. 12778 filed by Adam Kramer on November 27, 1931, in the United States District Court at Baltimore, Maryland. Adam identified his residence as 104 South Curley Street, Baltimore, and his occupation as mechanical engineer. He reported being born on December 8, 1903, in Schweinfurt, Germany, and marrying Sue Kramer on July 27, 1929, in Ellicott City, Maryland. The petition records Sue's birthplace as Deal Island, Maryland, and states that she resided with him. Adam reported Laufach, Germany, as his last foreign residence and his arrival in New York on September 29, 1926, aboard the Derfflinger. He reported having no children at the time. The witnesses were Matilda Wiethger and Mrs. Sue Kramer. The petition was part of the naturalization proceedings that resulted in Adam becoming a United States citizen on May 19, 1932.
