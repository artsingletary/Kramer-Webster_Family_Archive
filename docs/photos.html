Daniel Family Home – 2010 Walbrook Avenue, Baltimore

Photograph of the former Daniel family residence at 2010 Walbrook Avenue, Baltimore, Maryland, taken in the early 21st century. Mary Catherine Wilson Daniel (1859–1910) was living at this address when she died on October 4, 1910, according to her death notice published in The Baltimore Sun on October 5, 1910. The photograph shows the three-story rowhouse with boarded windows and doors and visible deterioration. The photograph's exact date and the building's subsequent history have not yet been established.

People: James Fairfax Daniel Sr. , Mary Catherine Wilson

Julia Ann Daniel

Gravestone of Julia A. Daniel (1812–1893) at Saint Johns Cemetery, Deal Island, Somerset County, Maryland. Jacob Parks

Travers Daniel

Gravestone bearing the name Travers Daniel (1780–1861) at Daniel Family Cemetery, Wenona, Somerset County, Maryland. The fragments were photographed after the original gravestone had fallen and deteriorated. The cemetery contains several Daniel family burials, and none of its historic gravestones remain standing, according to firsthand family observations.

People: Travers Daniel

Gabraella Rowe

Gravestone of Gabraella Rowe (February 27, 1854 – September 30, 1868) at Daniel Family Cemetery, Wenona, Somerset County, Maryland. The inscription is heavily weathered but appears to preserve her birth and death dates. Family genealogical records identify her as the daughter of Gabriel H. Rowe and Eleanor Ellen Daniel.

People: Gabraella Rowe
