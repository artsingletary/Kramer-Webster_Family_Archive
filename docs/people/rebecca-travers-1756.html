Biography

Rebecca Travers was born around 1756, according to FamilySearch genealogical information. Her exact birth date and birthplace remain unverified. Family histories associate her early life with Maryland, including Charles County and the Eastern Shore.

According to Richard Walser's genealogical account, Jeremiah Adderton and Some of His Descendants, Rebecca left Maryland as a teenager with Jeremiah Adderton, a widower who had five children from his earlier marriage to Ann Price. The couple traveled down the Potomac River through Virginia before settling in the Yadkin River region of Rowan County, North Carolina.

Rebecca and Jeremiah Adderton reportedly had two sons, Jeremiah Ira Adderton Jr. and John Adderton. Jeremiah's will was dated January 5, 1779, and recorded in Salisbury, North Carolina, on May 4, 1779. The probate account identifies Rebecca as having remarried by the time the will was recorded.

On April 5, 1779, Rebecca married James Daniel in Rowan County, North Carolina. Their marriage bond identifies her as Rebecca Atherton, while a historical marriage listing identifies her as Rebecca Travers and describes her as a widow. Family genealogical research identifies Travers as her maiden surname and Adderton as her surname from her first marriage.

According to the Daniel family genealogy, Rebecca and James Daniel had nine children: Travers, Woodson, Elizabeth, James, Mary (Polly), Rebecca, Nancy, Jayphus, and Poindexter Daniel. These relationships are supported by family lineage research, although the underlying evidence for each child should be reviewed individually.

FamilySearch records Rebecca's death year as 1815. Her exact death date, location, and burial place remain to be verified.

Sources

  1. Daniel Family — Genealogical History
    Type: family_history

    Privately compiled family genealogy titled The Daniel Name, probably prepared by Daniel Leroy Drake, son of Anna Wilson Daniel Drake and grandson of James Fairfax Daniel. The author identifies Anna Wilson Daniel as his mother, describes personally knowing James Fairfax Daniel, and references family records in his possession. The genealogy traces the Daniel family from Rowan County, North Carolina, to Deal Island, Maryland, and documents later generations of the Daniel, Drake, Webster, and related families. The latest date identified in the document is 1977, suggesting compilation or revision no earlier than that year. Copies were circulated among family members. According to family recollection, a copy was held by the Kramer-Webster family during the 1980s and certainly before 1993. The exact author attribution, compilation date, and original distribution date have not been independently verified.

    Location: Rowan County, North Carolina; Somerset County, Maryland

    Supports: Compiled genealogy of James Daniel and Rebecca Travers Adderton, Identification of Travers Daniel as their son, Marriage of Travers Daniel and Mary Wallace, Daniel family migration and connections between North Carolina and Maryland, Family relationships across subsequent Daniel generations
