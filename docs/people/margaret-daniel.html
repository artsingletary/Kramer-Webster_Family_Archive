Biography

Margaret Daniel was born approximately in 1818, probably in Somerset County, Maryland. She was a daughter of Travers Daniel (1780–1861), as identified in his last will and testament dated August 14, 1858, and proved May 24, 1861.

On June 3, 1834, Margaret Daniel married John Dix in Somerset County, Maryland, according to a historical marriage record. She was approximately sixteen years old at the time, based on the age recorded on her gravestone.

Margaret died on October 15, 1888, in the seventieth year of her age. She was buried at St. John United Methodist Church Cemetery on Deal Island, Somerset County, Maryland, where her gravestone identifies her as Margaret Dix.

Additional research is needed to establish John Dix's birth and death dates, the couple's children, and Margaret's exact birth date.
