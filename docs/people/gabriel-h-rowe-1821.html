Biography

Gabriel H. Rowe was born on October 5, 1821, in Somerset County, Maryland, according to family genealogical records. He was a son of George H. Rowe (1783–1844) and Nancy Webster (1797–1847), as identified in the family tree.

Gabriel married Eleanor Ellen Daniel on November 27, 1843, in Somerset County, Maryland, according to Ancestry family-tree information. Eleanor was a daughter of Travers Daniel and Mary L. Wallace. The original marriage record has not yet been examined.

The family tree identifies four children of Gabriel and Eleanor: Emily J. Rowe, Gabraella Rowe, Pinkney C. Rowe, and Gabriel Rowe. These relationships require verification through historical records.

Gabriel was a brother of Louisa Elizabeth (Lizzie) Rowe, who married James Travers Daniel. These marriages connected the Rowe and Daniel families across two generations.

Gabriel died on February 15, 1871, according to family genealogical information. He is recorded as buried in Daniel Family Cemetery in Wenona, Somerset County, Maryland. His exact burial location within the cemetery has not been established.
