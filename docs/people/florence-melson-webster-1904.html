Biography

Florence Melson Webster was born in 1904, the daughter of William Gabriel Snead Webster and Addie Leola Daniel. She appears in the family's 1910 United States Federal Census household in Somerset County, Maryland, at age six, where her name was transcribed as Florence N. Webster. She died in 1979.
