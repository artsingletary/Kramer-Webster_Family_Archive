Biography

Noah Kelly was born around 1802, according to FamilySearch genealogical information. His parents and exact birthplace have not yet been verified.

FamilySearch identifies Noah as the husband of Elizabeth Daniel, daughter of Travers Daniel and Mary L. Wallace. Their marriage is recorded in the family tree as July 13, 1825, in Coventry Parish, Rehobeth, Somerset County, Maryland. The original marriage record has not yet been examined.

The family tree identifies nine children of Noah and Elizabeth: Sara Annie, Louisa Elizabeth, William D., Mary C., Elmira, Julia A., James Emory, Noah Travers, and Susan Kelly. These relationships require confirmation through historical records.

Noah's death is reported as occurring in 1869. His exact death date, location, and burial place remain to be established.
