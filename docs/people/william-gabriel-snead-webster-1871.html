Biography

William Gabriel Snead Webster was born on June 29, 1871 in Somerset County, Maryland. He was the son of Gabriel S. Webster and Susan A. Windsor, according to family research. During his adult life, he was commonly known as Gabriel Snead Webster or simply Snead Webster. His gravestone records his birth year as 1871, although his 1939 death certificate incorrectly reports a birth date of June 29, 1877.

On December 23, 1899, Snead Webster and Addie Leola Daniel applied for a marriage license in Somerset County, Maryland. The application recorded Snead as 27 years old and Addie as 18. By June 1900, they were living together as husband and wife in Tangier District, Somerset County, where they were enumerated in the United States Federal Census.

Snead and Addie established their family on Deal Island in Somerset County, Maryland. On October 5, 1900, Snead acquired property on Deal Island through a deed involving Noah W. Webster, Rose A. Webster, John F. Webster, and Lucy B. Webster. The deed recorded a consideration of $115. Additional land records document the family's property transactions, including Addie's purchase of land from Lazarus B. Wilson and Annie B. Wilson in 1906 and a conveyance by Snead and Addie to Talmage D. Mister in 1909.

Snead and Addie were the parents of seven known children: Maynard Snead Webster (1900–1959), Susie Catherine Webster Kramer (1902–1993), Florence Melson Webster Adams (1904–1979), Charles Daniel Webster (1909–1960), Mary Catherine Webster Jackson (1916–1995), James Wilson Webster (1920–2005), and Eugene Lee Webster (1921–1998). The family lived on Deal Island during the early years of their marriage before relocating to Baltimore, Maryland. A surviving historical photograph documents their former home on Deal Island, which has since been demolished.

The Webster family was living in Baltimore by 1920. On April 26, 1922, Snead's wife Addie died at age 40, leaving several children still young. Her death certificate identifies Gabriel S. Webster as the informant. Family accounts describe the difficulties he faced caring for the younger children following her death. Later census records document different living arrangements among the children. In 1930, Charles and James Wilson Webster were living with their father, while Eugene Lee Webster was living with his older sister Florence and her husband Vernon L. Adams. The precise circumstances of these arrangements remain under investigation.

Gabriel later married Eloise Lonora Chiveral Reese, although the date of their marriage has not yet been established. His 1939 death certificate identifies Eloise as his wife and records his occupation as a carpenter, noting that he had retired from WPA employment.

William Gabriel Snead Webster died on April 17, 1939, at 200 North Glover Street in Baltimore, Maryland. His death certificate, completed using information supplied by Eloise, contains several discrepancies. It incorrectly identifies him as George Gabriel Snead Webster and names his parents as George Webster and Mary Katharine. Family research identifies his parents as Gabriel S. Webster and Susan A. Windsor. The certificate also reports a birth year of 1877, conflicting with the year 1871 inscribed on his gravestone, which was placed by his children.

Gabriel's death notice appeared in The Evening Sun of Baltimore on April 19, 1939. Funeral services were scheduled for April 20 at his residence on North Glover Street, followed by burial at Woodlawn Cemetery in Baltimore. His gravestone bears the inscription 'GABRIEL S. WEBSTER, 1871–1939, FATHER.'
