Biography

Gabraella Rowe was born on February 27, 1854, according to her gravestone at Daniel Family Cemetery in Wenona, Somerset County, Maryland.

Family genealogical records identify Gabraella as the daughter of Gabriel H. Rowe (1821–1871) and Eleanor Ellen Daniel (1828–1892). Eleanor was a daughter of Travers Daniel and Mary L. Wallace, making Gabraella their granddaughter. These relationships are recorded in the family tree and require confirmation through historical records.

Gabraella died on September 30, 1868, at approximately fourteen years of age. She was buried at Daniel Family Cemetery in Wenona, Maryland, where her gravestone was photographed before the cemetery's historic markers deteriorated.
