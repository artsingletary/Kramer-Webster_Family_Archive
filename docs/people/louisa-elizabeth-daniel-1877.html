Biography

Louisa Elizabeth Daniel, known as Emma, was born on October 30, 1877, on Deal Island in Somerset County, Maryland. Her 1939 death certificate identifies her parents as James Daniel and Mary C. Wilson, corresponding to James Fairfax Daniel and Mary Catherine Wilson.

Emma married William E. Kelly, whose surname also appears as Kelley in historical records. William was born in 1870, according to their shared gravestone. The date and location of their marriage have not yet been established.

By 1910, Emma and William were living with their children at 2014 Walbrook Avenue in Baltimore, Maryland. Emma's father, James Fairfax Daniel, lived nearby at 2010 Walbrook Avenue. The neighboring residences document the geographic closeness of two generations of the Daniel family after leaving their roots on Deal Island.

Emma was living at 520 Eutaw Street in Baltimore at the time of her death on September 16, 1939. Her death certificate records her cause of death as carcinoma of the uterus. She was buried at Woodlawn Cemetery in Baltimore, where she shares a gravestone with William E. Kelly, who died in 1950.
