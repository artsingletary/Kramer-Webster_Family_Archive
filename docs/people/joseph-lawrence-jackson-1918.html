Biography

Joseph Lawrence Jackson Jr., known as Lawrence, was born on September 23, 1918, in Baltimore, Maryland. His World War II draft registration identifies his mother as Elva M. Jackson.

Lawrence served in the United States Army Air Corps during World War II, attaining the rank of Technical Sergeant. National Archives prisoner-of-war records identify him as a member of a heavy bomber organization serving in the European theater.

During World War II, Lawrence was held as a prisoner of war by Germany. A National Archives record dated July 12, 1944, documents his POW status. A subsequent report dated June 12, 1945, records his status as returned to military control, liberated, or repatriated. His precise capture and liberation dates remain to be established.

Following the war, Lawrence returned to Baltimore. His September 27, 1945, draft registration lists his residence as 2927 Clearview Avenue and indicates that he expected to return to employment with Continental Can Company.

Lawrence married Mary Catherine Webster (1916–1995). Their marriage date remains to be established. A shared gravestone records his death in 1992.
