Biography

Elizabeth Daniel was born around 1810, according to family genealogical records. She was a daughter of Travers Daniel and Mary L. Wallace, whose family lived in Somerset County, Maryland.

Elizabeth married Noah Kelly on July 13, 1825, according to a historical marriage listing on page 43 that records the couple as 'Kelly Noah' and 'Daniel Elizabeth.' FamilySearch identifies the marriage location as Coventry Parish, Rehobeth, Somerset County, Maryland, although that precise location is not stated on the supplied record page.

The family tree identifies nine children of Elizabeth and Noah Kelly: Sara Annie, Louisa Elizabeth, William D., Mary C., Elmira, Julia A., James Emory, Noah Travers, and Susan Kelly. These names and relationships remain to be verified against historical records.

Elizabeth is identified as the deceased daughter of Travers Daniel in his last will and testament, dated August 14, 1858, and proved May 24, 1861, in Somerset County, Maryland. The will provides an inheritance for her children, establishing that Elizabeth had died before the will was executed and that she had surviving descendants.

Family genealogical information places Elizabeth's death in 1858. Her exact death date and burial place remain unknown.
