Biography

Robert Adam Kramer was born on November 5, 1936, in Baltimore, Maryland, the son of Adam Johannes Kramer and Susie Catherine Webster Kramer. He was recorded as Robert A. Kramer, age three, in the 1940 United States Federal Census. He died on October 3, 2012, in Greenville, Pitt County, North Carolina.

  1. 1940 United States Federal Census – Adam Kramer Family
    Type: census

    The 1940 United States Federal Census records the household of Adam Kramer (age 36), his wife Sue C. Kramer (age 36), and their children Robert A. Kramer and Betty A. Kramer. The family was enumerated on April 10, 1940, in Enumeration District 3-79, Sheet 7A, lines 34–37.
