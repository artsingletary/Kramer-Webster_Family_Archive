Biography

John Webster married Leah White on September 6, 1808, in Coventry Parish, Somerset County, Maryland, according to the parish marriage register.

FamilySearch identifies him as John Gabriel Webster III (1784–1844). His middle name, suffix, and birth and death years have not yet been independently verified.

FamilySearch identifies John and Leah as the parents of Gabriel Snead Webster (1822–1871), but this relationship requires additional historical documentation.
