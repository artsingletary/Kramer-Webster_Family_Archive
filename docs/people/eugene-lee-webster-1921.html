Biography

  1. 1930 United States Federal Census – Vernon and Florence Adams Families
    Type: census

    The Fifteenth Census of the United States, 1930, records two related Adams households on Linwood Avenue in Baltimore City, Maryland, enumerated April 4, 1930. Lines 18–21 list Vernon L. Adams (age 30), his wife Florence Adams (age 26), their son Lee Adams (age 6), and Eugene Webster (age 8), Florence's younger brother. Eugene's relationship is recorded as 'Brother' in the census. The immediately following household, beginning on line 22, lists John H. Adams (age 71) and his wife Chessie Adams (age 67), identified through family information as the parents of Vernon L. Adams. The census documents Eugene Webster residing with his older sister Florence and her family following the death of their mother, Addie Leola Daniel Webster, in 1922. The two Adams households were enumerated consecutively, providing evidence that Florence and Vernon lived next to Vernon's parents.

  2. 1940 United States Federal Census – Vernon and Florence Adams Family
    Type: census

    The Sixteenth Census of the United States, enumerated April 6, 1940, records the household of Vernon L. Adams (age 40), his wife Florence M. Adams (age 36), their sons Vernon L. Adams Jr. (age 16) and Kenneth W. Adams (age 9), and Florence's younger brother Eugene Lee Webster (age 18). The family was residing in Baltimore City, Maryland. The census indicates that the household members were living in the same house in 1935. Eugene had also been recorded living with Florence and Vernon in the 1930 census, documenting his continued residence with his older sister's family. The precise street address remains to be verified.

  3. 1950 United States Federal Census – Vernon and Florence Adams Family
    Type: census

    The Seventeenth Census of the United States, enumerated April 19, 1950, records the household of Vernon L. Adams (age 50), his wife Florence M. Adams (age 46), their son Kenneth W. Adams (age 19), and Florence's younger brother Eugene Lee Webster (age 28), beginning on line 6. The family was residing on Elsrode Avenue in Baltimore City, Maryland. Eugene had previously been recorded in Florence and Vernon's household in the 1930 and 1940 federal censuses. These three census records document Eugene's residence with his older sister's family over a period spanning twenty years, although they do not establish continuous residence throughout that period. The 1950 census also provides evidence that the Adams family was living on Elsrode Avenue by April 1950.

    Location: Elsrode Avenue, Baltimore City, Maryland, United States
