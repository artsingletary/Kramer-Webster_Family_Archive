Biography

Edward Maynard Kelly was born on October 3, 1898, on Deal Island in Somerset County, Maryland, according to family tree information recorded on Ancestry. He is identified as a son of William Edward Kelly and Louisa Elizabeth Daniel, known as Emma.

Edward's family later lived in Baltimore, Maryland. His parents were recorded at 2014 Walbrook Avenue in 1910, near his maternal grandfather, James Fairfax Daniel.

Ancestry family tree information gives Edward's death year as 1966. His exact death date and place remain to be verified.
