Biography

Adam Johannes Krämer was born on December 8, 1903, in Schweinfurt, Bavaria, Germany. In United States records, his surname was written as Kramer. He married Susie Catherine Webster. The couple appears in the 1930 United States Federal Census in Baltimore, Maryland, and in the 1940 census with their children Robert and Betty. Adam died in December 1969 in Baltimore, Maryland, and was buried at Woodlawn Cemetery in Baltimore on December 4, 1969.
