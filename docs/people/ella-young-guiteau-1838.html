Biography

Ella Young Guiteau was born in Maryland around 1838. She was a daughter of the Reverend Sheridan Guiteau and a granddaughter of Dr. Thomas Kelso, a Baltimore philanthropist.

Ella married William Daniel (1826–1897), a Baltimore attorney, Maryland legislator, and the Prohibition Party's candidate for Vice President of the United States in 1884. Their marriage date and the names of any children remain to be established.

Following William's death in 1897, Ella remained his widow. She spent nearly two years in Ocean City, Maryland, seeking improved health, according to her obituary.

Ella died on March 5, 1906, in Ocean City, Maryland. Her funeral was arranged from the residence of her sister, Mrs. Theodore Cook, at 914 North Charles Street in Baltimore. The Reverend De Witt M. Benham of Central Presbyterian Church officiated, and Ella was buried at Green Mount Cemetery in Baltimore.
