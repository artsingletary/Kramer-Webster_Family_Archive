Biography

Mary Catherine Webster was born in 1916, the daughter of William Gabriel Snead Webster and Addie Leola Daniel.

Following the death of Addie in 1922, Mary Catherine Webster was placed in the Kelso Home for Girls in Baltimore, Maryland, by her father, Gabriel Snead. The 1930 United States Census records Mary Catherine as a resident of the institution at age 14. Her exact admission date remains unknown. According to family recollections shared by Sue Webster's daughter, Mary Catherine found her experience at the home traumatic. Her sister Sue was deeply upset with their father over his decision to place Mary Catherine there.
