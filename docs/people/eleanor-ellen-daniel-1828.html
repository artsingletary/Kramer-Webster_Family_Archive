Biography

Eleanor Ellen Daniel was born around 1828, according to family genealogical records. She was a daughter of Travers Daniel and Mary L. Wallace, whose family lived in Somerset County, Maryland.

Eleanor married Gabriel H. Rowe on November 27, 1843, in Somerset County, Maryland. A historical marriage index records the bride as 'Eleanor Daniels,' preserving a variant spelling of her maiden surname.

Family genealogical records identify four children of Eleanor and Gabriel: Emily J. Rowe, Gabraella Rowe, Pinkney C. Rowe, and Gabriel Rowe. These relationships have not yet been independently verified against original records.

Their daughter Gabraella Rowe died on September 30, 1868, at approximately fourteen years of age and was buried in Daniel Family Cemetery in Wenona, Somerset County, Maryland.

Eleanor's husband Gabriel died on February 15, 1871, and is also recorded as buried in Daniel Family Cemetery. Eleanor reportedly died in 1892, although her exact death date and burial location have not yet been established.
