Biography

William Daniel Kelly was born on October 4, 1900, in Somerset County, Maryland, according to Ancestry family tree information. He was identified as a son of William Edward Kelly and Louisa Elizabeth Daniel, known as Emma.

William's parents later resided at 2014 Walbrook Avenue in Baltimore, Maryland, according to the 1910 United States Census. His maternal grandfather, James Fairfax Daniel, lived nearby at 2010 Walbrook Avenue.

Ancestry family tree information records William's death year as 1972. His exact death date and place remain to be verified.
