Biography

Jacob Parks was born on August 3, 1801, according to the inscription on his gravestone at Saint Johns Methodist Church Cemetery on Deal Island, Somerset County, Maryland.

Family genealogical information identifies Jacob Parks as the husband of Julia Ann Daniel, daughter of Travers Daniel and Mary L. Wallace. Their marriage date and location have not yet been established through a historical marriage record.

Jacob died on September 29, 1885, and was buried at Saint Johns Methodist Church Cemetery, Deal Island, Somerset County, Maryland.
