Biography

James Daniel was born around 1752, according to FamilySearch genealogical information. Other lineage research places his birth around 1755 in Northumberland County, Virginia. His exact birth date and birthplace remain to be established.

James Daniel lived in Rowan County, North Carolina, during the American Revolutionary War. He is recognized as a Revolutionary War patriot by the Daughters of the American Revolution (DAR Ancestor A029613) and through an approved lineage application to the Sons of the American Revolution (SAR). The DAR recognizes his patriotic service for furnishing money and supplies in North Carolina during the Revolution. Family genealogical accounts also describe service as a lieutenant in the North Carolina militia, but this military service has not yet been independently verified against original military records. His DAR ancestor record references North Carolina Revolutionary War accounts as evidence of his patriotic contributions.

On April 5, 1779, James married Rebecca, the widow of Jeremiah Adderton, in Rowan County, North Carolina. Their surviving marriage records identify her as Rebecca Atherton or Adderton, while another historical marriage listing uses the name Rebecca Travers. Family genealogical research identifies Travers as her maiden surname.

According to the Daniel family genealogy, James and Rebecca had nine children: Travers, Woodson, Elizabeth, James, Mary (Polly), Rebecca, Nancy, Jayphus, and Poindexter Daniel. These relationships are also represented in lineage research, although individual parent-child connections should be verified against supporting historical records.

The Daniel family genealogy reports that James served in public office in Rowan County, including service associated with the county courts and tax collection. The underlying court records should be examined to establish the dates and nature of these appointments.

James died on February 11, 1817, in Rowan County, North Carolina, according to the family genealogy and lineage research. His descendants later settled on Deal Island in Somerset County, Maryland, including the family of Travers Daniel and his son James Travers Daniel.

Further research is needed to locate and examine the original North Carolina Revolutionary War financial records cited in the DAR ancestor documentation. These references include North Carolina Revolutionary Army Accounts, Volume XII, Book A, Part XII, page 1631, and Accounts of the United States with North Carolina for the Revolutionary War, Book C, page 151, Roll S.115.45. The original entries have not yet been examined.

  1. Daniel Family — Genealogical History
    Type: family_history

    Privately compiled family genealogy titled The Daniel Name, probably prepared by Daniel Leroy Drake, son of Anna Wilson Daniel Drake and grandson of James Fairfax Daniel. The author identifies Anna Wilson Daniel as his mother, describes personally knowing James Fairfax Daniel, and references family records in his possession. The genealogy traces the Daniel family from Rowan County, North Carolina, to Deal Island, Maryland, and documents later generations of the Daniel, Drake, Webster, and related families. The latest date identified in the document is 1977, suggesting compilation or revision no earlier than that year. Copies were circulated among family members. According to family recollection, a copy was held by the Kramer-Webster family during the 1980s and certainly before 1993. The exact author attribution, compilation date, and original distribution date have not been independently verified.
