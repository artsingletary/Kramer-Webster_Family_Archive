Biography

Charles Daniel Webster was born in 1909, the son of William Gabriel Snead Webster and Addie Leola Daniel. He was recorded as Charles D. Webster, age nine months, in the family's 1910 United States Federal Census household in Somerset County, Maryland. He died in 1960.

  3. 1930 United States Federal Census – Adam Kramer and Snead Webster Families
    Type: census
