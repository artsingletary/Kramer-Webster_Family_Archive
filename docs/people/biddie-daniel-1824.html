Biography

Biddie Daniel was a daughter of Travers Daniel and Mary L. Wallace. Her father's 1858 will identifies her as Biddy Mister, providing direct evidence of her relationship to Travers Daniel and her married surname.

Her Baltimore death certificate records a birth date of October 15, 1824, in Maryland. The certificate identifies her father as 'Daniel Mister,' born in North Carolina, and her mother as Mary Wallace, born in Maryland. The father's name appears to contain an error, but the names and birthplaces are consistent with the family identification supported by Travers Daniel's will.

Biddie married Severn Mister, who died before her. Their marriage date has not yet been established.

Biddie died on February 6, 1904, in Baltimore, Maryland. Her death certificate records her age as 79 years, 3 months, and 22 days, while her death notice in The Baltimore Sun reports her age as 80. The newspaper identifies her residence as 1021 Linden Avenue.

Her death certificate records burial at Loudon Park Cemetery in Baltimore, with burial scheduled for February 8, 1904. The burial entry should be checked against cemetery records.
