  2. 1910 United States Census — Daniel and Kelley Families
    Type: census

    Thirteenth Census of the United States, 1910, Baltimore City, Maryland, enumerated April 22, 1910. James F. Daniel, age 54, resided at 2010 Walbrook Avenue with his wife, Mary C. Daniel, age 50, and their children James F. Daniel, age 24, Annie W. Daniel, age 22, and Wilson B. Daniel, age 12. James F. Daniel was recorded as a retail oyster dealer. His daughter Emma Daniel Kelley resided nearby at 2014 Walbrook Avenue with her husband and children. The neighboring households document the proximity of two generations of the Daniel family in Baltimore.
